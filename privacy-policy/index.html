Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Convertza ("we", "our", or "the app") handles information when you use the app on your device. By using the app, you agree to the practices described below.

1. Overview

Convertza is a document conversion tool (PDF, images, text). The app is designed to respect your privacy: your documents are processed locally on your device and are never uploaded to any server.

2. Local processing and file access

  • All conversions happen on-device. We do not operate servers that receive, store, or process your documents or images.
  • Files are selected through the system document picker (Storage Access Framework). Convertza does not request any storage permission and cannot read files you have not explicitly chosen.
  • Converted files and imported documents remain under your control on your device.

3. Data collected and stored on your device

  • Conversion history: a history of your past conversions is stored locally on the device and can be deleted at any time from the app.
  • Settings: your preferences (language, theme) are stored locally.
  • Advertising counter: a local counter is used to limit ad frequency. It contains no personal data.

We do not collect personal identifiers such as your name, email address, or phone number.

4. Advertising

Convertza displays advertisements provided by third-party platforms, notably Google AdMob. Advertising platforms may use device identifiers (such as the Google Advertising ID), approximate location, and other signals to serve and measure ads, in accordance with their own privacy policies.

Advertising Data: Google may use the advertising ID for ads measurement and may show personalized ads only when consent has been granted.

5. Consent (Google UMP) and GDPR/EEA users

If you are located in the European Economic Area (EEA), the United Kingdom, or Switzerland, or where required by law, Convertza uses the Google User Messaging Platform (UMP) to collect your consent before requesting and personalizing advertisements.

  • A consent form is shown when required and your choice is respected.
  • You can review or change your choices at any time via "Privacy Options" in the app's Settings screen.
  • If you do not grant consent, non-personalized (or limited) ads may be shown.

6. Third-party services

The app uses the following third-party services while respecting this policy:

  • Google AdMob (Google Privacy Policy) — advertising SDK.
  • Google User Messaging Platform — consent management.

7. Children

Convertza is not directed to children under the age of 16 (or the minimum age required in your country). We do not knowingly collect personal data from children.

8. Your rights

Depending on your location (in particular GDPR/EEA), you may have rights to access, rectify, or erase personal data, restrict processing, or object to processing. Since Convertza does not store personal data on servers, most of your data lives on your device and can be removed by deleting the app or resetting its data (e.g., clearing the conversion history).

9. Security

Because files are processed locally and no personal data is transmitted, the main data stored by the app remains on your device and is protected by your device's own security.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The "Last updated" date at the top of this page reflects the latest revision. You are encouraged to check this page periodically.

11. Contact us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, contact us at: support@example.com
